# TTRPG Tools

TTRPG Tools is the host and catalogue for browser-based tabletop role-playing tools.

## Language

**Reference table**:
The full rulebook table shown next to a roll's result, with the rolled row (or cell) marked, so the player sees the result in the context of every possible outcome. In Russian UI and docs: «справочная таблица».
_Avoid_: lookup table, results list

## Paper minis

**Calibration**:
The head and feet lines a player sets on a mini's artwork to give the figure its own height, so a raised weapon does not count toward it. One per mini, shared by its front and back.
_Avoid_: height lines, per-side calibration

**Calibration session**:
A player's draft of one mini's calibration in the calibration dialog, from opening it to Apply or Cancel. Only Apply saves it to the mini.
_Avoid_: calibration state, dialog state

**Base style**:
How a mini stands on the table, chosen once for a whole print: tent (folds into its own ⊥ stand), flat (folds in half and goes into a standee's slot or clip) or foamboard (front and back are glued to both sides of foamboard and cut out with it).
_Avoid_: mount, stand type, cut type

**Face**:
The paper that carries one side's artwork. A mini has a front face and a back face of equal height: the taller of the two images.
_Avoid_: side panel, half

**Tab**:
The strip at the outer end of a tent mini's face that the figure stands on. Its depth is half the base width.
_Avoid_: base strip, foot

**Floor strip**:
The strip two tabs deep under a tent mini's front tab. It folds under the stand and is glued to both tabs.
_Avoid_: base, bottom tab

**Fold**:
The line between the front and back faces where a tent or flat mini is folded in half.
_Avoid_: middle line, crease

**Tongue**:
The narrow strip under a flat or foamboard mini's face that goes into a standee's slot or clip. It is narrower than the face, so the face rests on its shoulders.
_Avoid_: tab, peg, insert

**Front sheet**:
A foamboard print sheet that carries the front faces and the cut marks.
_Avoid_: odd page, face page

**Back sheet**:
A foamboard print sheet that carries the back faces, laid out as the mirror of its front sheet so each back lands behind its front through the board.
_Avoid_: reverse page, even page

**Registration mark**:
A cross in each corner of a front sheet and its back sheet, used to line the two sheets up on the foamboard.
_Avoid_: alignment mark, crop mark

**Export**:
The zip of prepared paper minis that a batch upload restores.

**Layout**:
The arrangement of minis on sheets for printing and cutting.

**Printer scale**:
The fraction a printer applies when it prints a page with "Scale to Fit". Paper minis enlarges its PDF by the inverse of this value so figures print at their intended size.

**Strip**:
A full-width horizontal band of a sheet that can be cut off with one straight cut.

**Column**:
A mini-width part of a strip, separated from its neighbours by straight vertical cuts.

**Stack**:
Minis placed one above another within a column, no taller together than the strip.

**Shared cut line**:
The single cut between two abutting minis. Columns, stacked minis and strips sit edge to edge with no gap, and their cut marks stay on their own pieces.
